隱私權政策

適用於 Emberline: Last Haven 2.0.0。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日。

開發者與隱私聯絡人：方士軒（Shih-Hsuan Fang）。信箱：a820628a@gmail.com。

遊戲處理哪些資料

遊戲不要求建立帳號，沒有廣告、分析、跨 App 追蹤或內購。遊戲程式不蒐集或傳送姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人、廣告識別碼或遊玩紀錄，也不將這些資料提供給第三方。

旅程進度、補給、行車紀錄、原作封存與音效等設定保存在裝置的本機儲存空間。原生版本隨 App 提供遊戲資源，遊玩不需要下載遠端關卡。

匯入、匯出與刪除

只有在你主動選取檔案時，遊戲才會讀取 JSON 存檔或支援的灰燼匯出代碼，匯入內容不會上傳。匯出會產生由你自行保管的本機檔案。存檔保留到你清除遊戲資料為止，沒有開發者端的雲端副本。

Android 可在系統設定清除本 App 的儲存資料；iOS 可選擇「刪除 App」清除本機資料，「卸載 App」會保留資料。網頁版可清除此網站的網站資料。匯出的備份檔須自行刪除；作業系統備份是否保留副本，依你的系統備份設定與平台政策而定。

主動聯絡客服

若你寄信詢問問題，開發者會收到你的寄件地址、信件內容及主動附上的檔案，僅用於處理詢問與相關往返。請勿寄送密碼、憑證或不必要的個人資料。客服信件保留至問題處理及必要後續聯絡完成；你可來信要求刪除，依法必須保留的部分除外。電子郵件會經由郵件服務商處理。

網站與平台

本政策與客服頁沒有加入分析或追蹤程式。開啟公開網頁時，網站主機服務商仍會處理提供頁面所需的網路請求；App Store、Google Play 與作業系統也可能依各自政策處理下載、診斷及備份資料。

Privacy Policy

Applies to version 2.0.0. Updated October 20, 2026. Developer and privacy contact: Shih-Hsuan Fang (方士軒), a820628a@gmail.com.

Data used by the game

The game has no account registration, advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking or in-app purchases. The game code does not collect or transmit your name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifier or gameplay records, or share those data with third parties.

Campaign progress, supplies, journey logs, imported legacy records and settings are stored locally. The native app includes game resources and does not need to download remote levels.

Import, export, retention and deletion

The game reads a JSON save or supported Ashes export code only when you choose to import it. Imported data are not uploaded. Export creates a local file under your control. Saves remain until you clear the game's local data; the developer has no cloud copy.

On Android, clear this app's storage in system settings. On iOS, use Delete App; Offload App retains app data. For the web version, clear the site's website data. Delete exported backup files separately. Operating system backups depend on your backup settings and the platform's policies.

Support requests

If you email the developer, your sender address, message and files you choose to attach are used to address the inquiry and related correspondence. Do not send passwords, credentials or unnecessary personal data. Support correspondence is retained until the inquiry and necessary follow-up are complete. You may request deletion, subject to legal retention duties. Email is processed through email service providers.

Website and platforms

These pages contain no analytics or tracking scripts. The website host processes network requests needed to serve public pages. App Store, Google Play and operating system services may process downloads, diagnostics and backups under their own policies.

The game contains apocalyptic themes, weapon combat and horror or suspense and is not directed at children. Contact the email above for privacy or deletion inquiries. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
