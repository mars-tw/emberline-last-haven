怎麼遊玩

  • 護航：按住左右鍵操舵，等敵人攀車後點「甩尾清車」。射擊會自動進行。
  • 守點：觀察西側、中央與東側屍群，再決定投餌位置。誘餌消耗旅程共用的補給。
  • 守夜：觀察乘客與冷窗，封存判讀；依窗側或車頂的可見訊號選擇反制。
  • 點右上「行車簿」可暫停、匯出旅程或調整音效與動態。離開畫面後，回來需手動繼續。

存檔與離線

原生 App 內含遊戲資源，可離線遊玩。網頁版須先完成首次載入與離線快取。進度保存在本機，不會自動跨裝置同步。請在行車簿選擇「匯出完整旅程」保留備份，重新安裝或清除資料前先保管匯出的檔案。

若無法保存，請確認裝置仍有可用儲存空間，並依遊戲提示匯出旅程。若畫面或聲音異常，可先暫停後重新開啟 App，再回報發生步驟；不要為了排除問題直接清除資料。

Controls

  • Convoy: hold left or right to steer. Use drift after enemies latch onto the vehicle. Shooting is automatic.
  • Defense: observe the west, center and east approaches, then choose a bait position. Bait consumes the campaign's shared supplies.
  • Night watch: observe the passenger and window, commit your reading, then counter the visible window-side or roof threat.
  • Open the journey book at the top right to pause, export your campaign or change sound and motion settings. Resume manually after returning.

Saves and offline play

The native app includes game resources for offline play. The web version needs an initial load and completed offline cache. Progress is local and does not sync automatically between devices. Export your full campaign from the journey book before reinstalling or clearing data.

If saving fails, check available device storage and export as prompted. If visuals or sound fail, pause and reopen the app, then report the steps. Avoid clearing data as an initial troubleshooting step.

The game contains fictional apocalyptic threats, shooting, horror and suspense. It is not a children's traffic education app. There is no login, advertising, in-app purchase or gameplay data upload. See the Privacy Policy for data deletion and support correspondence.
